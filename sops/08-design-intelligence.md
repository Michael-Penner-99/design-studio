# SOP 08 — Design Intelligence

> Substep 1 of Creative Direction: pick the visual style.

## Purpose
Decide the visual style stance for the site (vibe + archetype-driven) — the one decision that the rest of the design hangs on.

## Inputs
- `clients/{slug}/brand/brand-dna.md`
- `clients/{slug}/research/competitors.md`
- The Capstone reference page at `templates/pages/home.html`

## Steps

1. **Surface three candidate styles** appropriate for the contractor's archetype and sub-trade:
   - Owner-led residential warm
   - Premium-family
   - Commercial-industrial
   - Modern minimalist
   - Local hero
   (Pick three from the menu above. Justify each in two sentences using brand-dna evidence.)

2. **Differentiate from competitors.** Read each competitor section in `research/competitors.md` and note the visual stance they each take. The contractor's chosen style must visibly differ from at least 3 of 5 competitors.

3. **Commit to one.** Write a single paragraph to the top of `strategy/design-direction.md` under `## Style stance`. State the chosen style and why.

4. **Sound check.** Re-read voice.md. If voice says "warm and neighborly" but the chosen style is "commercial-industrial", the choice is wrong. Reconcile before advancing.

## Outputs
- `strategy/design-direction.md` (Style stance section)

## Exit criteria
- One style chosen and justified in writing.
- Differentiation from competitors named.
